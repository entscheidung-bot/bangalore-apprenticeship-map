Apprenticeship paths for technical, scientific, hands-on skills.

A practical map for an adult learner. Prioritizes dedicated, work-based or lab/shop-floor programs. Separates continuous routes from vacancy-driven recruitment.

Age reality: no credible Bengaluru program found that promises “no age limit.” National apprenticeship rules set a minimum age of 14, 18 for hazardous work; the cited rule summary does not state a general upper limit.[2] Individual employers commonly impose their own caps. Treat “no upper cap published” as a lead, not eligibility. Confirm against the live vacancy notice before investing time.

Best routes

1. CMTI — manufacturing R&D

Best fit: precision engineering, automation, additive manufacturing, metrology, materials.

Government manufacturing R&D institute in Yeshwanthpur. Its stated domains include smart manufacturing/Industry 4.0, sensors and controls, additive processes, precision metrology, materials testing, nano-manufacturing.[7]

Dedicated options: recurring 2–5 day professional programs; 15–30 day customized training; planned 3–6 month finishing schools; 2-month summer internships; apprenticeship page.[4][5][7]

Access: short-program route is strongest for a career changer. CMTI's indexed training calendar confirms FY 2026–27 activity.[6] Age requirement not published on the program page; enquire directly.

Prospect: manufacturing quality, test/calibration, CNC/automation, AM, industrial R&D. Build portfolio: measured part, process sheet, inspection report.

2. NATS — graduate/diploma OJT

Best fit: engineering, computer/electronics, laboratory/industrial technical roles.

Government NATS is structured practical on-the-job training for graduate, diploma and vocational certificate holders, normally 6–12 months.[1] Employers on the portal have training managers, modules and facilities; completion yields a Government Certificate of Proficiency.[1]

Access: qualification-gated. Use Bengaluru/Karnataka vacancies; apply only when role and notice match. It pays a stipend, but explicitly does not guarantee employment after training.[1]

Prospect: strongest signal is employer-specific experience plus certificate. Convert it to job evidence: supervisor reference, project artefact, documented tools.

NATS portal

3. NAPS / Apprenticeship India — trade + employer route

Best fit: hands-on trades, service operations, IT support, emerging employer-defined roles.

Under the Apprentices Act, training combines basic training and workplace OJT.[2] Designated trades are mostly engineering/manufacturing; optional trades let employers define roles in engineering, technology, services and more.[2]

Access: national portal listings are vacancy-driven. Freshers may receive basic training before shop-floor OJT; government guidance lists ITIs, NSDC centres, PMKKs, polytechnics and qualifying industry centres as potential basic-training providers.[2]

Prospect: choose an establishment with actual equipment, named mentor, assessment, realistic job family. Avoid titles with no stated curriculum or worksite.

Apprenticeship India

4. Karnataka DITE — local employer discovery

Best fit: finding Bengaluru companies, ITIs and state routes.

Karnataka’s Department of Industrial Training & Employment maintains a Bengaluru Division list of apprenticeship-training industries, plus Craftsman Training, upgraded ITI/Udyoga and short-course links.[3]

Access: directory, not an offer. Use it to create a shortlist, then check each employer's current notice/portal entry. This is the most practical local discovery layer.

Prospect: trade-specific local placement network; useful beside NAPS rather than instead of it.

Scientific route: practical answer

For science, do not chase a generic “science apprenticeship.” In Bengaluru, target a technical laboratory function: materials characterization, calibration/metrology, sensor/test systems, manufacturing quality, nano-manufacturing, or process development. CMTI publicly lists relevant laboratories and services, including measurement/calibration, aerospace qualification, material/metallurgical testing and nanomaterial characterization.[7] Pair this with a NATS role only if your degree/diploma matches.[1]

90-minute application protocol

  1. Pick one track. Do not apply across unrelated tracks.
  2. Make accounts on NATS and Apprenticeship India.
  3. Open Karnataka’s Bengaluru directory. Select 10 employers matching your track.[3]
  4. For each vacancy, record: age cap, qualification, duration, stipend, worksite, mentor, curriculum, conversion history.
  5. Apply only where every essential field is stated. Call/email CMTI for live schedule, eligibility, fees, equipment time.

Decision standard

Prefer: paid OJT + named equipment + named mentor + assessment + certificate. Reject: tuition-only “apprenticeship,” no worksite, no curriculum, vague placement claim, pressure to pay quickly.
